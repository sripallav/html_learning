Understanding Colspan vs Rowspan Visually

1. Normal 2x2 Grid (No Span)

Every row has exactly 2 separate cells:

Cell 1 Cell 2
Cell 3 Cell 4

2. With COLSPAN="2" (Horizontal Stretch →)

Cell 3 and Cell 4 became ONE single wide cell stretching across 2 columns:

Cell 1 Cell 2
I am ONE cell with colspan="2" (Stretching Left to Right)

3. With ROWSPAN="2" (Vertical Stretch ↓)

Cell 1 stretched down across 2 rows, so the second row only needs ONE cell:

I am ONE cell with rowspan="2"
(Stretching Top to Bottom) 		Cell 2 (Row 1)
Cell 4 (Row 2)
