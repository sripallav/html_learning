3. Text Formatting Tags

Strong tag: This text is critically important (bold).

B tag: This text is just visually bold.

Em tag: This text has emphasis (italic).

I tag: This text is in italics.

Mark tag: This word is highlighted like with a marker pen.

Small tag: © 2026 Sri Pallav. All rights reserved (fine print).

Del and Ins (Great for e-commerce pricing):
Old Price: ₹1,999
Offer Price: ₹999

Sub (Subscript): Water formula is H2O.
Sup (Superscript): Math formula is (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2.
