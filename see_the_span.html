Why do we need <span>?

Case 1: The Mistake - Using <div> inside a sentence

Chudu, sentence madyalo <div> pedithe line cut aypoyi new line loki velipoyindi:

Your order status is

DELIVERED
Thank you for ordering with Swiggy!


Case 2: The Solution - Using <span> inside a sentence

Ippudu chudu, <span> valla sentence line cut avvaledu, okate line lo neat ga undi:

Your order status is DELIVERED . Thank you for ordering with Swiggy!
